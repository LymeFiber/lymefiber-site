Service Plans & Pricing (Draft)

This page describes available service plans and associated costs.

Internet and Phone Services

INTERNET SERVICES: LymeFiber offers a choice of Internet speeds. View the Internet Broadband Fact Labels Here. Unlike cable modem or DSL service, LymeFiber Internet service is symmetrical – that means you can send files and videos at the same speed as you receive them! LymeFiber representatives will be happy to advise you as to the Internet service level most appropriate to your needs. You are not locked in to a particular service level, and we have no contracts. You may upgrade – or downgrade – at any time with a simple phone call or email to support@Notices.LymeFiber.net.

PHONE SERVICES: LymeFiber phone services are a comprehensive package of functions that include unlimited local and long distance calling plus several calling features.  Voicemail is $3.00 per month and it includes the option of receiving your voicemails as an attachment to your email. There is a one-time telephone activation fee of $30 per line.

TELEPHONE NUMBER PORTABILITY: Keeping your current telephone number(s) is not a problem. LymeFiber asks that you DO NOT discontinue your current telephone service. We will handle the disconnection of your old line while transferring the number to our service. Telephone user guides for residences and businesses will be sent via email once your lines have been transferred.

If you choose phone service, you will receive a Letter of Agency (LoA) form to be completed and returned to us – or you can download it here from our site.  Note that in order to move your existing phone number from your old provider to LymeFiber, your account with your old provider must be current and in good standing. Transferring the telephone number can take 10 days or more.
