LymeFiber was created to bring reliable high-speed internet to communities that have been underserved for years. Our network supports working from home, online learning, telehealth and modern home technology, and we take pride in serving our neighbors with responsive, local support.

Our basic service delivers **100 Mbps symmetrical speeds for $72 per month**, with plans up to **1 Gigabit per second**. Optional telephone service, which can replace your Consolidated or Topsham bill, is **$25 per month**. See [Plans & Pricing](/plans/).

## Why households choose LymeFiber

- Symmetrical upload and download speeds
- No data caps or throttling
- Locally built and operated
- Reliable service during peak hours and storms
- Optional telephone service for $25 per month

## Orford

Fiber internet is now available in select areas of Orford, north from the Lyme/Orford town line:

- River Road north to the Route 10 intersection, including Sanborn Lane
- Route 10 north to 712 Dartmouth College Highway
- Roads connecting to Route 10: Tree Farm Road to the end of the line, Sawyer Brook Road to the end of the line, Boat Landing Road (pole line on the road), and Hebb Drive to the end of the line
- Route 25A east from Route 10 to 2959 Route 25A
- Route 25A (Bridge Street) west from Route 10 to the Connecticut River
- Archertown Road east to Tannery Road, and Tannery Road
- 709 and 712 Route 10, Bellmore Drive
